About

Field survey

Trained as a land surveying engineer and specialised in geomatics and geospatial data processing, I work where geospatial data, remote sensing and machine learning meet.

Since 2022 at Siradel, I have been designing and industrialising MLOps pipelines for semantic segmentation of aerial and satellite imagery, and developing Python pipelines to automate the production of geographic data. I also serve as Technical Product Owner for the industrialisation of production processes.

Before that, I spent four years at the CNRS / University of Rennes 1 Topo-bathymetric LiDAR Research Platform. I processed airborne point clouds at scale, produced DTMs and DSMs, and worked on machine-learning 3D classification and full-waveform data. This work took me to a wide range of sites, from Swedish Lapland to the Loire River.
