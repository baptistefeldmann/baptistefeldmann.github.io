Grid A1

plateforme_lidar

Python library and scripts developed at the LiDAR Research Platform to process airborne topo-bathymetric data. LAS/LAZ I/O (laspy 2), full-waveform data, SBET trajectories, CloudCompare and LAStools wrappers, 3DMASC classification, bathymetric correction, water surface extraction, overlap control and deliverable generation. No longer maintained. Development has been taken over by Paul Leroy, research engineer at the LiDAR Platform, as lidar_platform.

Grid A2

bibliotheque

A family library manager: scan a book's barcode with your phone and a Python worker fills in the record from public catalogues (BnF, Google Books, Open Library). The Supabase (Postgres) database enforces access rules with RLS. Enrichment runs on GitHub Actions, and natural-language search relies on an LLM at constant cost, whatever the size of the collection.
